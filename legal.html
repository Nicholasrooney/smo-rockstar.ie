The Small Print

Cookies &
Privacy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

This site is run by SMO, a band based in Dublin, Ireland. This page explains what we store on your device and what happens to any personal details you give us. We've kept it in plain English rather than legalese.

Cookie Policy

We use as little as we can get away with. Nothing that identifies you is stored until you either add something to your basket or buy something.

Essential — always on

These are needed for the site to work at all, so they don't require consent.

NameTypeWhat it doesHow long
smo_cart Local storage Remembers what's in your basket between pages. Never leaves your browser until you check out. Until you clear it or complete an order
smo_cookie_consent Cookie Remembers the choice you made on the cookie banner, so we stop asking. 12 months

Analytics — only with your consent

If you press Accept All, we load Google Analytics so we can see roughly which pages people read and which shows get clicks. IP addresses are anonymised. If you press Essential Only, it never loads and no analytics cookies are set.

We use Google Analytics 4. It sets cookies beginning _ga that recognise a returning browser for up to two years. It tells us page views, roughly where in the world visitors are, and which links get clicked — not who you are.

NameTypeWhat it doesHow long
_ga Cookie Tells returning visits apart from new ones. Set by Google Analytics. 2 years
_ga_22X3PYDM1J Cookie Keeps track of a single visit (session state) for this site's Analytics property. 2 years

None of these are set unless you press Accept All, and IP addresses are anonymised before Google stores them.

Payments

Card payments are handled by Stripe on Stripe's own secure page. Your card number is never entered on, sent to, or stored by this website. Stripe sets its own cookies on that page, which are covered by their privacy policy.

Changing your mind

You can reopen the cookie banner at any time to change your choice, or clear cookies in your browser settings.

Privacy Policy

What we collect, and when

  • If you buy merch: your name, email address and delivery address. You enter these on Stripe's checkout page; Stripe passes them back to us so we know where to post your shirt.
  • If you just browse: nothing personal. Your basket stays in your own browser.
  • Card details: we never receive them. Stripe handles the payment and only tells us whether it succeeded.

What we do with it

Order details are emailed to the band and kept in a private order log on the server so we can pack and post your order, and sort out any problems afterwards. That's the only thing we use them for. We don't sell your details, we don't share them with advertisers, and we won't add you to a mailing list off the back of an order.

Who else sees it

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Hostinger — hosts the website and the order log.
  • Google — Analytics, and only if you consented.
  • An Post / courier — gets the delivery address so your order arrives.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for six years, because Irish tax law requires us to hold on to sales records for that long. Your cookie choice lasts 12 months.

Your rights

Under GDPR you can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (where we're not legally required to keep it). Email us and we'll sort it within a month. If you're not happy with how we've handled it, you can complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission.
