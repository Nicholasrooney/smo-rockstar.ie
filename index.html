The Band

Raw Sound.
Real Energy.

SMO is Sam and the band — a full-tilt alt-rock outfit out of Dublin. Equal parts swagger and sincerity, they've been turning heads at venues across the capital with a sound that hits before you've even settled in.

From late nights at the Grand Social to packed floors at Whelans, the SMO live show is built to be felt. New releases dropping constantly — the story's only getting started.
